## Ergonomics and Posture: Why Slouching in a High Chair Matters

Most parents think about safety when choosing a high chair, but posture deserves just as much attention. How your baby sits during meals directly affects their ability to chew, swallow safely, and stay focused at the table.

### Proper Sitting Posture

Feeding therapists and pediatric occupational therapists consistently recommend the 90-90-90 rule: hips at 90 degrees, knees at 90 degrees, and ankles at 90 degrees. This neutral, upright posture prevents slouching, supports core engagement, and keeps the airway open for [safe swallowing](https://momcozy.com/blogs/babycare/choose-safe-baby-high-chair "safe swallowing"). A baby who is slumped or unsupported is at a higher risk for gagging and discomfort during meals.

### Footrest Necessity

Dangling feet might look harmless, but they create real problems. When a child's feet have no surface to press against, their core muscles have to work harder just to stay upright. This leads to fatigue, distraction, and slouching, exactly the posture you want to avoid. [A footrest](https://momcozy.com/blogs/babycare/do-babies-need-high-chair-footrest "A footrest") gives babies and toddlers the grounding they need to sit tall and focus on eating.

### A Custom Fit at Every Stage

The DinerPal features both an adjustable seat depth and an adjustable footrest, so you can fine-tune the fit as your child grows. The seat can be positioned forward or back to support different body proportions, while the footrest slides up or down to keep feet flat and supported. Getting this right takes less than a minute and makes a noticeable difference in how comfortably and attentively your child sits.
