# Momcozy Baby Swing Troubleshooting Guide: Power, Motion, Music, Child Lock & Seat Mode Fixes

Most Momcozy baby swing problems can be narrowed down by checking the power connection, timer, child lock, movement path, and selected mode. This baby swing troubleshooting guide explains what to check when the swing will not turn on, stops moving, or does not respond to the controls. Remove your baby before making adjustments, and stop using the swing if you notice damage or unstable movement. If the problem continues, review the product **specifications and available support options or compare other baby swing models**.

## Quick Checks Before You Troubleshoot

These checks resolve many control and motion problems without disassembling the swing.

**Issue:** The swing is not operating normally.

**Possible cause:** Loose power, an expired timer, child lock, an obstruction, or the wrong mode.

**How to fix:**

1. Confirm that the outlet works, the recommended adapter is fully connected, and the cord is undamaged.
2. Unlock the controls if the child-lock indicator is on.
3. Check the timer and clear the seat’s full movement path.
4. Confirm whether the product is configured for swing mode or stationary seat mode. For stationary seat mode, unplug the unit and remove the toy bar so a larger child has enough room to sit comfortably.

**Safety note:** Remove your baby before checking assembly or changing the backrest. Never adjust the backrest while your baby is in the seat.

**Related guide:** See **How to Use Baby Swings** for assembly, controls, and daily operation.

## Baby Swing Won’t Turn On

A swing that shows no lights or response usually needs a power-path check before anything else.

**Issue:** The panel stays dark.

**Possible cause:** No outlet power, a loose or unsuitable adapter, damaged power parts, or stationary seat mode.

**How to fix:**

1. Test the outlet with another device and reconnect the adapter at both ends.
2. Confirm that the adapter matches the specification in the manual; do not substitute an unapproved adapter.
3. Check the cord and plug for cuts, bent parts, overheating, or other damage.
4. Contact Momcozy Support if the swing remains unresponsive.

**Safety note:** Do not use the swing in a damp area, operate it with damaged power parts, or attempt an electrical repair.

## Swing Stops Moving or Does Not Swing

First clear the movement path and check the timer; an obstruction can trigger motor stall protection.

**Issue:** The seat does not move or stops.

**Possible cause:** An obstruction may trigger motor stall protection, or the timer may have ended.

**How to fix:**

1. Turn the swing off, remove your baby, and clear the complete movement path.
2. Press the power button to restart it.
3. Check the timer and select a new duration if the previous setting ended.
4. Contact support if stops continue with no obstruction or timer issue.

## FAQs

### Q1: Why won’t my Momcozy baby swing turn on?

Check that the outlet works and that the recommended adapter is firmly connected at both ends. Inspect the cord and plug for visible damage. The product should remain unplugged in stationary seat mode. If the panel stays dark after these checks, stop troubleshooting and contact Momcozy Support.

### Q2: Why did my baby swing stop moving?

The timer may have ended, or an obstruction may have triggered motor stall protection. Clear the path, check the timer, and restart.

### Q3: How do I unlock the Momcozy baby swing control panel?

Check whether the child-lock indicator is on. Press and hold the music and volume buttons together until the indicator changes, then test another button. If the panel repeatedly locks or remains unresponsive after the correct unlock procedure and a normal restart, contact Momcozy Support for further assistance.

### Q4: Why can’t I change the music or swing mode?

Child lock may be preventing the controls from responding, so check the lock indicator first. After unlocking the panel, select one motion mode and wait for the new pattern to begin before changing the speed or sound. Avoid pressing several controls at once. Restart the swing once if needed, and contact support if the same buttons repeatedly fail.

### Q5: Can I use the swing while it is in stationary seat mode?

**No**. Do not plug in or use powered swing functions in stationary seat mode.

### Q6: Can I wash the baby swing seat cover?

**Yes**. The zip-off cover is machine washable, but follow the manual’s wash and drying instructions. Wipe the frame, toy bar, and toys instead.

### Q7: When should I stop using swing mode?

Stop using Swing Mode when your baby tries to climb out, which commonly begins around nine months, or reaches 19.5 lb (9 kg), whichever comes first.
