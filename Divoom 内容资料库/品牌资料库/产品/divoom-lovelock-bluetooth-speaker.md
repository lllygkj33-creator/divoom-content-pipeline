## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What is Divoom Lovelock best for?

Divoom Lovelock is best for personal music, meaningful gifts, bedside listening, desk audio and shared music moments. Its love-lock inspired design, compact size and voice memo function make it especially suitable as a thoughtful gift.

### Can I record a voice message with Lovelock?

Yes. Lovelock supports short voice memo recording for up to 60 seconds, allowing you to leave a personal message before gifting it or use it for small audio notes.

### Can two Lovelock speakers be paired together?

Yes. Lovelock supports TWS pairing with another Lovelock speaker, so two units can be used together for a wider stereo listening experience.

### How long does the battery last?

Lovelock offers up to about 5 hours of music playback, depending on volume level, audio content and usage conditions.
