# Divoom Emodo Pixel Badge + Times Frame Bundle

**Divoom Emodo Pixel Badge + Times Frame Bundle** \- wear your pixel art on the go, then showcase it on a floating transparent smart desk display. Two app-controlled Divoom devices that turn any desk into a living pixel-art gallery.

- **Floating Transparent Display:** The 10.1" IPS 1080p Times Frame uses a transparent panel to create a striking 3D-like depth effect for photos, videos, clock faces, and pixel art.

- **Always-On Desk Companion:** Times Frame runs on wired 5V/2A power for an always-on display, while the Emodo badge recharges for wearable, on-the-go pixel art.

- **Two Devices, One Bundle:** Get the wearable Emodo badge and the Times Frame smart display together - a ready-to-give gift for geeks, gamers, and pixel-art lovers.
