## PDP 描述（官方商品描述）

The Divoom Fairy-OK is a retro-styled mini karaoke speaker with a built-in wireless microphone and pixel art LED display. Its compact vintage design makes it a fun, portable singing companion for parties, bedroom karaoke sessions, road trips, and everyday entertainment.

It also doubles as a Bluetooth speaker for everyday music playback. A delightful gift for kids, teens, and anyone who loves singing — whether at home, outdoors, or on the go.

## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What is Divoom Fairy-OK?

Divoom Fairy-OK is a retro-style mini karaoke speaker with a microphone, designed for casual singing, music playback, home entertainment, small gatherings and gift moments.

### Is Fairy-OK a speaker or a karaoke microphone?

Fairy-OK works as a compact karaoke speaker set. It combines speaker playback with a handheld microphone, making it suitable for relaxed karaoke, music fun and everyday entertainment rather than professional stage performance.

### What is included in the Fairy-OK package?

The package includes the main speaker, microphone, microphone cover, transparent suction cup, packaging box, carrying bag, charging cable and user manual. Please check the final package list on the product page before purchase.

### Can I use Fairy-OK for home karaoke?

Yes. Fairy-OK is suitable for home karaoke, family music time, small parties, casual singing practice and short video-style entertainment. It is designed for fun daily use, not professional studio recording.
