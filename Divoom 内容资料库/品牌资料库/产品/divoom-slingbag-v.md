## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What is Divoom Sling Bag-V?

Divoom Sling Bag-V is a pixel art LED sling bag designed for everyday carry, crossbody styling and creative self-expression. It combines practical storage with a customizable LED display, making it suitable for streetwear, travel, city walks and daily outfits.

### Can I customize the LED display?

Yes. Sling Bag-V supports LED pixel display customization through the Divoom app. You can create, upload or choose pixel-style visuals to match your outfit, mood or personal style.

### Does Sling Bag-V come with a power bank?

No. A mobile power supply is not included with Sling Bag-V. You need to prepare your own compatible power bank to power the LED display during use.

### What can I carry in Sling Bag-V?

Sling Bag-V is designed for small daily essentials such as a phone, wallet, keys, earbuds, small accessories and a power bank. If you need to carry larger items, please check the storage layout and product size before ordering.
