## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What is Divoom Pixoo Slingbag?

Divoom Pixoo Slingbag is a compact LED sling bag with a 4.7-inch pixel display. It is designed for daily carry, streetwear styling, travel, festivals and creative self-expression.

### Can I customize the LED display?

Yes. You can use the Divoom app to create, upload or choose pixel-style visuals for the LED screen, including icons, animations and personal designs. This makes the bag more expressive than a regular sling bag.

### Does Pixoo Slingbag need a power bank?

Yes. The LED display needs a compatible power bank for power. The front pocket is designed to store a power bank while using the LED display. Please prepare your own suitable power bank before use.

### What can I carry in Pixoo Slingbag?

Pixoo Slingbag is suitable for small daily essentials such as a phone, wallet, keys, earbuds, small accessories and a power bank. It is designed for lightweight daily carry, not large-capacity storage.
