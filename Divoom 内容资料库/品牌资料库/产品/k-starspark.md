## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What is K-StarSpark best for?

K-StarSpark is designed for casual karaoke, family music nights, parties and creative gift moments. It combines a microphone speaker with playful karaoke modes and a pixel-style display, making it suitable for home entertainment rather than professional stage performance.

### How many karaoke modes does K-StarSpark have?

K-StarSpark features 8 karaoke modes, including Original, Robot, Child, Singer, Studio, Popular, Host and Electro. These modes help create different singing effects for family fun, parties and casual performances.

### Can I use K-StarSpark with my phone?

Yes. K-StarSpark is designed to work with a phone for music playback and karaoke control. Please follow the product manual for the correct connection method and supported app features.

### Is K-StarSpark a speaker or a microphone?

K-StarSpark is a karaoke microphone speaker. You can hold it like a microphone while using it for singing, music playback and casual entertainment.
