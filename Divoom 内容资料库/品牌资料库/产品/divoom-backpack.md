Pixoo Backpack-M is a smart LED backpack with a built-in 16×16 pixel display. It is designed for daily carry, school, commuting, travel and creative outfit expression.

Yes. You can use the Divoom app to create, upload or choose pixel-style artwork and animations for the LED backpack display. App functions may vary depending on device and system version.

Yes. Pixoo Backpack-M includes a separate laptop compartment that can accommodate up to a 15-inch laptop. Please check your laptop size before purchase if you use a thicker protective case.

The backpack includes a laptop compartment, a spacious main compartment and 5 hidden pockets to help organize books, daily essentials, tech accessories, wallet, keys and smaller items.

Incredibly fun backpack to take to the con!

I opted to get the 16x16 pixel display of this bag over higher pixels because it stays more true to the concept of pixel art. Having a higher pixel screen would give you a blurry-looking image unless you stand far away. With the 16x16 pixel screen, you can stand a few feet away and get a sense of what the artwork is.

That said, how well you're able to interpret the image greatly depends on the quality of the artwork. 16x16 pixel means you have to stretch your creativity to be ...Show full reviewable to convey the subject matter with the least amount of pixels available. Given that Divoom is a pixel art community, you can test out a bunch of artwork to see what style works best to display on this bag. I would highly suggest creating pixel art with black background. This would help the artwork to stand out more and be easier to read.

Onto the display and the bag. The display seems very nice with no curvature or dead pixels. The screen is protected by a translucent plastic cover which reduces the sharpness of each square pixel. When the pixels light up, each of them has a bit of vignetting (dark areas around corners) making certain artwork difficult to discern. However, this brings me back to the previous point about stretching your creativity. Good pixel art will display fine despite pixel vignetting and still be easy to discern what the subject matter is. Again, artwork with black background reads best on this screen.

The bag's main zipper is made with a very nice quality water-resistant zipper. Unfortunately, the zipper was not installed properly, making it very difficult for the zipper to round the corners of the bag. This is because there's not enough room given when installing the zipper so it doesn't have the room to travel smoothly.

The bag doesn't have much width to fit bulky stuff but the height of the bag makes up for it. The external flap along with the water-resistant zipper makes me feel better about not getting the power bank wet.

I do wish that there was a button that can turn off the screen. Depending on your power bank, you have to unplug the USB cable from the power bank because it will not turn off if there is a device that keeps drawing power. If there was an on/off button for the screen, you won't have to open up the bag and unplug the power bank each time you need to turn it off.

Lastly, I'm very happy with this bag. Now that I'm going to more cons and running an artist alley table, this Divoom bag will be a very good walking advertising banner and a way to brighten up my booth and set it apart from others. I do wish there was a scrolling text feature in the app but making my animated pixel art seem very easy. Highly recommended.
