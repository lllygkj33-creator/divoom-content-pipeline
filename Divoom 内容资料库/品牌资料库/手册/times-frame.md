## How to Set Up Wi-Fi Connection?

EN

1. Download and install the Divoom app on your smartphone.
2. Connect your phone to a Wi-Fi network and keep the phone close to the device and router.
3. Plug in the power cable. The screen will prompt for Wi-Fi setup.
4. Open the app → Go to My Page → Tap My Devices → Switch WiFi → Tap Add Device.
5. Follow the in-app instructions, enter your Wi-Fi password, then tap Next.
6. The app will start pairing, and the device will display “Connecting to Network.”
This takes about 10–20 seconds.
7. When pairing is successful, the app and device will both show confirmation.

FAQ

## 1. Device is unresponsive / stuck in restart loop:
a) Confirm the USB power adapter supports 5V=2A
b) Try using a different USB cable and power adapter
c) Ensure no buttons were pressed during power-up

## 2. Network configuration fails:
a) Make sure the device and phone are near the router
b) Open networks without a password are not supported
c) Try a different phone or Wi-Fi network
d) Ensure you're using the latest version of the Divoom app

## 3. Troubleshooting:
a) If you cannot connect to Wi-Fi, check signal quality. Keep both device and phone near the router.
b) If a firmware update is in progress after connection, tap Update in the app and wait patiently.
