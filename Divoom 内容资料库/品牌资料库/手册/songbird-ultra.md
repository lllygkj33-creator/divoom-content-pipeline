Pairing:
$\textcircled{1}$Speaker off.
$\textcircled{2}$Microphone off, then hold its power button for 10 seconds to enter pairing mode
(mic indicator flashes rapidly).
$\textcircled{3}$Activate the speaker and wait for 3 seconds.
$\textcircled{4}$Short-press the microphone's power button to complete pairing.

配对:
$\textcircled{1}$音箱关机;
$\textcircled{2}$麦克风关机状态下，长按10秒进行配对(麦克风指示灯快闪);
$\textcircled{3}$音箱开机，等待3秒后再操作第4步；
$\textcircled{4}$短按麦克风开机键，完成配对。
