## Button Function Diagram

- Turn left: Decrease volume
- Turn right: Increase volume

- Triple click: Previous track

## Tiivoo-2 Features
Click the joystick to enter menu, push it left and right to switch 10 major functions; Click the joystick to select the function, and press the button to return to parent menu.

## The menu functions are as follows:
1. Music modes: Bluetooth, TF card, USB.
2. FM radio: Manual station search with a built-in antenna for enhanced sensitivity.
3. Ambience: Multiple lyric backgrounds and text effects (requires music software to support lyric output).
4. Gallery: Connect to the app to upload images and project on the speaker screen.
5. Pomodoro: Uses the Pomodoro technique to improve work efficiency.
6. Alarm clock: Set alarms with timely reminders.
7. Tools: puzzle games, lucky spinner, timer, countdown, scoreboard, voice messages, etc.
8. Multiple clock faces: Built-in system displays time, date, etc.
9. White noise: Various white noise options including sounds of animals and nature, perfect for aiding sleep.

## How to connect to the Divoom App?
Search 'Divoom' application for both iOS and Android systems.
*Please grant all privilege requests prompted by the APP.
For Apple iOS system, connect 2 Bluetooth
Search and connect the 'Divoom Tiivoo 2-Audio' in Bluetooth settings for music, then search and connect the 'Divoom Tiivoo 2-Light' in the Divoom APP for controlling the screen.
For Android system, connect 1 Bluetooth
Search and connect the 'Divoom Tiivoo 2-Light' or 'Divoom Tiivoo 2-Audio' in APP to control the music and screen.

## FM Radio Mode
1. Press button and turn it right to the "FM Radio" menu
(*Manual search is required in first use).
2.Switch channels via button

## TF card mode
## Method 1:
Insert the TF card, and the audio should start to play automatically.

## Method 2:
Double-click the button to quickly switch the playback mode to TF mode.

## Method 3:
After short pressing the Joystick, select the music icon left and right to enter the music mode, and select the TF card icon.

*TF card maximum capacity:64GB
Only supports:MP3/WAV/FLAC

## Computer Speaker (USB) Mode
Connect the speaker to the computer via Divoom charging cable, automatically switch to USB sound card mode. For the first connection will install drivers automatically; then please manually select "Tiivoo" as the sound output device to enable computer speaker mode.

Note: When connected to a computer, the speaker may not charge, use, charge the speaker fully in advance or lower the volume operation.
