## HOW TO USE Ditoo-5M'S MENU FUNCTION?

1. Short press the ' m ' menu key to enter the menu system;
2. Use the ' ♥ ' keys to switch between music/pixel coloring/tools/alarm clock/game;
3. Pull down the joystick to confirm the option, and short press the ' m ' menu key to exit the current menu function.

## 【Music】
Pull down the joystick to enter the music function, press ' ♥ ' keys to switch to Bluetooth or TF card mode, pull down the joystick to confirm.

## 【Pixel coloring】
Pull down the joystick to start coloring, press the ' + - ' ♥ ' key to move the icon, pull down the joystick to fill in the selected area until the color of the pattern is filled, then enter the next pixel image coloring.
*Press the (sun icon) key to view the complete pattern prompt

## 【Tool】
Pull down the joystick to enter the tool, and press the ' ♥ ' keys to select tools: scorer, timer, countdown, noise detection

## 【Alarm clock】
Pull down the joystick to enter the alarm clock settings, press the '+ - ' keys to set the clock and minute time respectively, pull down the joystick to confirm and start the alarm clock*How to cancel alarm: Enter the alarm clock setting interface and long press the joystick to delete the set alarm clock

## 【Game】

## HOW TO USE THE UAC (USB Audio Card) ?
1. Connect the USB-C cable from your PC to the speaker. The speaker will
auto-switch to UAC mode.
2. If no sound, please manually choose "Divoom Audio" on your PC as output.
Due to the varies outputs on the different computer USB port and audio
signals bandwidth, the supplied power may not be sufficient to meet the
power consumption at the higher/max volume. In such case, we suggest to
fully charge the speaker in advance or lower the speaker volume for a
longer sustainable audio playtime.

## HOW TO CHARGE?
1.Insert the USB Type-C cable into the speaker.
2.Press the any button to check the battery percentage.
When charging, the indicator light will lit. Upon 100%
charged, the indicator will be fully lit.

*Please use a 5V=2A charger.

## SAFETY GUIDE

## Please read the following instructions carefully:
1.Please follow our product installation about operation instructions.
2.Always place the speaker on a smooth and stable surface.
3.Please place the speaker on an open area for the best sound quality. Do not block the surroundings of the speaker.
4.Do not push any objects into speaker vents or slots. Do not apply any great force on the speaker.
5.Please keep the speaker at a room temperature environment. Do not please the speaker near any heat sources (such as radiators, heat registers, stoves, fire place, or direct sunlight). Do not place the speaker near any open flame source.
6.Do not attempt to service the speaker by yourself. Always refer to the instructions by qualified service personnel.
7.To avoid the risk of electrical hazard, do not expose this speaker to wet surface.
