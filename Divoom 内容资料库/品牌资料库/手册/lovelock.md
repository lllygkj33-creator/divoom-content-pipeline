## HOW TO CONNECT IN BLUETOOTH?
1. Power ON the speaker, and make sure the Bluetooth indicator light $ \textcircled{4} $
is flashing in blue.
2. Go to the phone’ s Settings > Bluetooth, and connect the
“ Divoom LoveLock ”.
3. Once connected, start to play music.
*If the Bluetooth indicator light $ \textcircled{4} $ shows solid blue (not flashing),
the speaker might be connected to a different phone/tablet.
You must disconnect the Bluetooth connection by double press on
button $ \textcircled{3} $.

## HOW TO ACTIVATE THE RECORDING FUNCTION?
1. Hold the record button $ \textcircled{1} $ to start the recording,the recording indicator
will remain solid.Release the button $ \textcircled{1} $ to stop the recording.
2. Press the record button $ \textcircled{1} $ to play and loop the recorded audio.
Press the button $ \textcircled{1} $ again to stop the playback.

## HOW TO CONNECT THE TWS MODE?
1.Power on a speaker, and connect it with a smartphone.
Hold the volume knob $ \textcircled{2} $ to activate the TWS mode.
2.Power on the other speaker, and hold its volume knob $ \textcircled{2} $
to activate its TWS mode.
3.The two speakers to start to pair. Once paired, the Bluetooth LED light $ \textcircled{4} $
will change from flashing to solid lit.
4.Hold the volume knob $ \textcircled{2} $ to disconnect the TWS mode.

## HOW TO CHARGE?
1.Connect the Type-C cable to the speaker’s charging port $ \textcircled{5} $.
2.When charging, the indicator $ \textcircled{5} $ will light up.
Once fully charged, the indicator $ \textcircled{5} $ will turn OFF.
3.Please use a 5V---1A charger to charge the speaker.

## SAFETY GUIDE
Please read the following instructions carefully:
1.Please follow our product installation about operation instructions.
2.Always place the speaker on a smooth and stable surface.
3.Please place the speaker on an open area for the best sound quality. Do not
block the surroundings of the speaker.
4.Do not push any objects into speaker vents or slots. Do not apply any great
force on the speaker.
5.Please keep the speaker at a room temperature environment. Do not please
the speaker near any heat sources (such as radiators, heat registers, stoves, fire
place, or direct sunlight). Do not place the speaker near any open flame source.
6.Do not attempt to service the speaker by yourself. Always refer to the
instructions by qualified service personnel.
7.To avoid the risk of electrical hazard, do not expose this speaker to wet
surface.
