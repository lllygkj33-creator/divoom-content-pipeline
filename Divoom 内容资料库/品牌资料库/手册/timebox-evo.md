## How to connect in Bluetooth
# How connect with the speaker/mobile application?

## Download mobile application
The Divoom application is available in both iOS and Android systems.
You can search for ‘Divoom’ or scan this QR code.
* Please note: iOS and Android connecting process is different

## ■ For Apple iOS system
1.In Settings, please turn on the Bluetooth.
2.Search and connect with the ‘Timebox-Evo-audio’.
3.In mobile application, search and connect with ‘Timebox-Evo-light’.

## ■ For Android system
1.Please turn on the Bluetooth in Settings.
2.Connect the ‘Timebox-evo-light’ and ‘Timebox-Evo-audio’ in the mobile application.

## How to record a voice memo (up to 60 seconds)?

1. Hold the play button to activate the voice memo function
2. Start to record yourt message
3. Press play button to end the recording, and press play button again to playback.
* Once played, the message will be erased.

---

## How to setup a manual alarm?
1. Hold the center ‘Auto power-off’ and enter the manual alarm mode
2. Press +/- to adjust time, press play button to confirm hour and switch to minute.
3. Press play button to activate alarm, or press ‘lighting’ button to disable existing manual
alarm and exit.
* Timebox-Evo support a single manual alarm (more alarm options available in mobile
application)

## How to setup the auto power off?
1.Press the ‘Auto Power-off’ button to activate Auto power off function.
2.Keep pressing the same button to rotate the power off time interval.
3.Release the ‘Auto Power-off’ at the desired time interval to confirm.
* Select '00 min' to disable power off and exit auto power off mode.

## How to charge?
■ Insert the USB-C cable into the USB port on
the rear panel.
■ When Timebox-Evo is ON or charging, presses
the power button to check the battery status.
■ With 100% battery level, Timebox-Evo can
provide up to 6 hours playtime.
