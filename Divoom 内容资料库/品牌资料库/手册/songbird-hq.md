To avoid cross-pairing your microphone with other speaker:
为防止麦克风与其他音箱串联,可设置配对麦克风和音箱:
1.Pairing: $\textcircled{1}$Turn on the speaker,and put it in music mode; $\textcircled{2}$Long
press the ON/OFF button of microphone for 10s when mic's OFF (til the
mic indicator blinks); $\textcircled{3}$Switch speaker into Karaoke mode; $\textcircled{4}$Short
press the ON/OFF button of microphone - Complete pairing.
1.配对:$\textcircled{1}$音箱开机,且为音乐模式;$\textcircled{2}$麦克风关机状态下,长按10秒进行配对
(麦克风指示灯快闪);$\textcircled{3}$打开音箱K歌模式;$\textcircled{4}$短按麦克风开机键,完成配对;

2.Unpairing: $\textcircled{1}$Turn on the speaker,and put it in music mode; $\textcircled{2}$Long
press the ON/OFF button of microphone for 10s when mic's ON (til the mic
indicator blinks);$\textcircled{3}$Switch speaker into Karaoke mode;$\textcircled{4}$Short press the
ON/OFF button of microphone - Complete factory reset.
2.解除配对:$\textcircled{1}$音箱开机,且为音乐模式;$\textcircled{2}$麦克风开机状态下,长按10秒进行配对
(麦克风指示灯快闪);$\textcircled{3}$打开音箱K歌模式;$\textcircled{4}$短按麦克风开机键,恢复出厂设置。
