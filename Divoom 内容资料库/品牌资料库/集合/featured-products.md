### What are Divoom featured products?

Divoom featured products include pixel art speakers, smart desk displays, digital photo frames, pixel art backpacks, and creative desk gadgets designed to make modern spaces feel more personal and alive.

### Which Divoom product is best for a desk setup?

Ditoo-Pro and MiniToo are great choices for retro-inspired desks with music and personality. Times Gate is better suited to users who want a smart pixel dashboard, while Pixoo64Ⅱ is ideal for larger pixel art displays.

### Which Divoom products are good gifts?

MiniToo, Ditoo-Pro, and Tiivoo-2 are playful creative tech gifts for desk setup lovers, students, gamers, and anyone who enjoys retro-inspired design.

### Can every Divoom product be customized with the app?

App compatibility depends on the product. Compatible products may support pixel art, animations, widgets, or other customizable display content. Check each product page for specific details.

### What is the difference between Pixoo64Ⅱ, Times Gate, and Times Frame?

Pixoo64Ⅱ is designed for larger pixel art visuals. Times Gate works as a smart desk dashboard with multiple display panels. Times Frame focuses on photos, widgets, and personal digital content for modern desks and living spaces.
