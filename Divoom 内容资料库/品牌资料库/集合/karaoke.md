## FAQ About Karaoke Speakers

### Which Divoom karaoke speaker is best for beginners?

Fairy-OK and K-StarSpark are good starting points for casual singing, gifting, and everyday entertainment. They are compact, easy to use, and designed for relaxed karaoke moments rather than professional stage performance

### Which Divoom karaoke speaker is best for family karaoke?

SongBird-SE and Songbird-HQ are suitable for home karaoke, family gatherings, duets, and group entertainment. Both are designed with two wireless microphones, while Songbird-HQ provides a stronger 50W sound experience

### Which karaoke speaker is best for parties?

Spark-Pro is designed for parties with 80W sound, two wireless microphones, and an RGB light ring. SongBird-Ultra offers a more powerful 100W setup for larger gatherings, outdoor entertainment, and live music moments

### Does Divoom offer karaoke speakers with pixel displays?

Yes. Ditoo-Mic, Ditoo 5-Mic, and K-StarSpark combine karaoke features with pixel-style visuals or pixel displays. Check each product page for the exact display functions

### Do Divoom karaoke speakers include microphones?

Microphone configuration varies by model. Some products include one wireless microphone, while products such as SongBird-SE, Songbird-HQ, and Spark-Pro include two wireless microphones. Check the individual product page before purchasing

### Are Divoom karaoke speakers only for home use?

No. The collection includes compact speakers for bedrooms and casual use, family karaoke sets for home entertainment, and stronger party speakers for larger gatherings and selected outdoor scenarios
