## FAQ About Divoom Chargers & Desk Gadgets

### What products are included in Chargers & Desk Gadgets?

This collection includes Divoom charging essentials such as compact fast chargers, multi-socket chargers, and USB-C charging cords for daily desk and device use

### Which Divoom charger should I choose for a desk setup?

Dipow-65W is the better choice if you want to power multiple devices or build a more complete desk setup. Dipow 35W is better for compact everyday charging

### Is the USB-C Charging Power Cord useful as an add-on?

Yes. It works well as an extra or replacement cable for compatible devices and is a practical add-on for gift bundles, travel bags, and desk setups

### Are these products only for Divoom devices?

They are positioned as Divoom charging essentials, but compatibility depends on the product and device. Customers should check the product page and device requirements before purchasing

### Are chargers good for bundles or gifts?

Yes. Chargers and cables are useful add-ons when buying Divoom speakers, displays, or creative desk gadgets, especially for users building a complete setup

### Do I need a charger for Times Gate or Times Frame?

Some Divoom smart displays require stable power during use. Check each product page for the exact power requirements and included accessories before adding a charger
