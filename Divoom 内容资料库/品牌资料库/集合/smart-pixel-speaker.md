## FAQ About Pixel Art Speakers

### What is a pixel art speaker?

A pixel art speaker combines Bluetooth audio with a digital display for pixel art, clocks, animations, icons, and other creative visuals. Divoom pixel speakers are designed to add both sound and personality to desks, bedrooms, gaming setups, and everyday spaces

### Which Divoom pixel speaker is best for a desk setup?

Ditoo-Pro is a strong choice for a statement retro desk setup with 15W sound and a classic computer-inspired design. MiniToo is better for compact desks and bedside tables. Tiivoo-2 adds photo, lyric, and FM radio features, while Timebox-Evo is suited to users who want a 16×16 pixel screen with alarm and planner tools

### Can I customize the pixel display?

Compatible Divoom pixel speakers support customizable visuals through the Divoom App. Available options may include pixel art, animations, clocks, text, and other screen content depending on the model

### Which pixel speaker is best as a gift?

MiniToo, Tiivoo-2, and Ditoo-Pro are strong gift choices for desk setup lovers, students, gamers, music fans, and anyone who enjoys retro-inspired creative tech

### Do Divoom pixel speakers support Bluetooth?

The Pixel Art Speaker collection includes Bluetooth audio products. Playback options vary by model, so check the individual product page for Bluetooth version, USB audio, TF card playback, and other supported connections

### Are pixel art speakers only suitable for gaming rooms?

No. Pixel art speakers can also fit work desks, study desks, bedrooms, bedside tables, shelves, and gift setups
