## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Divoom?

Divoom creates smart displays, pixel art speakers, and creative tech accessories designed to bring more personality to your desk, room, gaming setup, and everyday moments. From customizable pixel visuals to immersive sound, each product adds a new layer of expression to your space.

### Which Divoom product is best for my match-day setup?

It depends on how you want to enjoy the moment.

- **Times Gate** is ideal for a futuristic desk or living-room dashboard with five customizable screens.
- **Times Frame** is a smart floating display for photos, countdowns, custom visuals, and ambient lighting.
- **Pixoo 64** is designed for bold pixel art, animations, and expressive room decor.
- **MiniToo** is a compact retro-style pixel speaker for music and desk setups.
- **Ditoo 5-Mic** adds music, pixel animations, and microphone-ready fun to smaller gatherings.
- **Songbird-HQ** is made for bigger watch parties with powerful sound and two wireless microphones.

### Can I customize the screen for match night?

Yes. Many Divoom products support custom pixel art, animations, text, clock faces, and display modes through the Divoom App. You can create visuals for kickoff countdowns, goal celebrations, team-inspired colors, or your own match-night mood.

Available features vary by product, so please check the individual product page before ordering.

### Do Divoom displays automatically update live football scores?

Some Divoom products support real-time data displays and sports-related widgets, but the available data sources and supported functions vary by device, region, and app version.

For a personalized match-night setup, you can also use custom pixel art, countdowns, animations, and text displays. Please review the product page and the latest Divoom App options for the exact features available on your device.

### Do I need the Divoom App?

The Divoom App unlocks many of the creative features available on supported devices, including pixel art, animations, custom text, display faces, and additional visual modes.

Some products can also be used for music playback or basic functions without the app, but the app is recommended for the full Divoom experience.
