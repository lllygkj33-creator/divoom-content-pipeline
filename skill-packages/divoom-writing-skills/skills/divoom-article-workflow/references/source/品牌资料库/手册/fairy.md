## Bluetooth mode:
1. Power ON speaker, and make sure indicator$\textcircled{1}$ is flashing Blue
2. Go to phone Settings>Bluetooth, please pair “Fairy-OK”
3. Once connected, you can play audio
*If the indicator$\textcircled{1}$ shows solid Blue, it is currently being connected by another phone. Please
disconnect its current connection by holding left$\textcircled{7}$ & right$\textcircled{8}$ buttons.

## TF card mode
1. Power ON speaker, and insert the TF card (indicator$\textcircled{1}$ shows red)
2. Turn the volume knob$\textcircled{6}$ to adjust volume, Press the same knob to play and pause
music, Press left button$\textcircled{7}$ or right button$\textcircled{8}$ to change song
*If the indicator$\textcircled{1}$ is showing a different color, please press the mode button$\textcircled{9}$ to change to the TF
card mode(red indicator)

## FM radio mode
1.Power ON speaker, and press mode button$\textcircled{9}$ to change to the FM radio mode
2.Turn the radio knob$\textcircled{10}$ to change station (Once it pick up a station, the indicator will
show green)
3.Turn the volume knob to adjust volume
*If the indicator$\textcircled{1}$ is showing a different color, please press the mode button$\textcircled{9}$ to change to the
FM mode(green indicator)
**You can mute the radio by press the play knob $\textcircled{12}$

## How to enable the karaoke function
Power ON the MIC, speaker will automatically try to pair the speaker. Once connected,
the speaker rear panel will light up, and the MIC will be ready to use.

## How to charge?

1.Insert the USB-C cable to charging port (rear panel). You can listen to music while charging, but it may affect the FM radio signal.

2. Charging time may vary by the USB charger. We suggests to use a 5V==2A USB charger.
