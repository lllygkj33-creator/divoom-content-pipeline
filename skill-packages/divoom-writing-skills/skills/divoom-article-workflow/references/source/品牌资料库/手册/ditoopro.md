## HOW TO USE DITOOPRO'S MENU FUNCTION?
1. Short press the (m) menu key to enter the menu system;
2. Use the (←)(→) keys to switch between music/pixel coloring/tools/alarm clock/game;
3. Pull down the joystick to confirm the option, and short press the (m) menu key to exit the current menu function.
【Music】
Pull down the joystick to enter the music function, press (←)(→) keys to switch to Bluetooth or TF card mode, pull down the joystick to confirm.
【Pixel coloring】
Pull down the joystick to start coloring, press the (+)(-)(←)(→) key to move the icon,
pull down the joystick to fill in the selected area until the color of the pattern is filled, then enter the next pixel image coloring.
*Press the (sun icon) key to view the complete pattern prompt
【Tool】
Pull down the joystick to enter the tool, and press the (←)(→) keys to select tools: scorer, timer, countdown, noise detection
【Alarm clock】
Pull down the joystick to enter the alarm clock settings, press the (+) (-) keys to set the clock and minute time respectively, pull down the joystick to confirm and start the alarm clock*How to cancel alarm: Enter the alarm clock setting interface and long press the joystick to delete the set alarm clock
【Game】
After selecting the game menu function, press the (←)(→) keys to switch between slot machine/dice/magic black 8 ball/pinball/flying bird/snake/pixel racing, etc, and pull down the joystick to confirm and start the game. During the game, short
press the (+)(-)(←)(→) keys to move up and down, and pull down the joystick to
confirm.

# HOW TO SET ALARM CLOCK MANUALLY?

## HOW TO SET ALARM CLOCK MANUALLY?
1. After pressing the (m) button, use the (←)(→) keys to switch to the alarm
clock, and pull down the joystick to enter the settings;
2. First press the (+)(-) keys to set the clock time, pull down the joystick to
confirm; then press the (+)(-) keys to set the minute time, pull down the joystick,
and a ticked alarm clock icon appears to indicate that the alarm clock is set
successfully.
*How to cancel alarm: Enter the alarm clock setting interface and long press the joystick to delete the
set alarm clock. A crossed alarm clock icon appears to indicate successful deletion.
*DitooPro only supports 1 set of manual alarm clocks. If you need more alarm clocks, please use
Divom App to set them.

## HOW TO PLAY THE TF CARD AUDIO?
1. Insert the TF card into the TF card slot, and the speaker will automatically
enter the TF card mode; 2. Short press the mode button $ \textcircled{8} $ to quickly switch the
playback mode;
3. Switch the mode: short press the (m) menu button, pull down the joystick to
enter "music" menu. Use the (←)(→) keys to switch to TF card mode, and pull
down the lever to enter.
*In music mode, please use the keyboard buttons and joystick controls to play music
*MP3/WAV/FLAC are the only supported file formats. The maximum supported capacity is 64GB.

## HOW TO CHARGE?

1.Insert the USB Type-C cable into the speaker.
2.Press the power button if you want to check the battery percentage.
3.When charging, the indicator light will lit. Upon 100% charged, the indicator
will turn off.

*Please use a 5V= 2A charger, and do not use higher
voltage charger.

## SAFETY GUIDE

Please read the following instructions carefully:
1.Please follow our product installation about operation instructions.
2.Always place the speaker on a smooth and stable surface.
3.Please place the speaker on an open area for the best sound quality. Do not
block the surroundings of the speaker.
4.Do not push any objects into speaker vents or slots. Do not apply any great
force on the speaker.
5.Please keep the speaker at a room temperature environment. Do not please the
speaker near any heat sources (such as radiators, heat registers, stoves, fire
place, or direct sunlight). Do not place the speaker near any open flame source.
6.Do not attempt to service the speaker by yourself. Always refer to the
instructions by qualified service personnel.
7.To avoid the risk of electrical hazard, do not expose this speaker to wet surface.
