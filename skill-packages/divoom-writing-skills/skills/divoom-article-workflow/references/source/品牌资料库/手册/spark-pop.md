## 蓝牙模式 Bluetooth mode

连接后，请开始播放音乐
Please set up Bluetooth > your phone,

*如果开机后指示灯显示蓝色而未闪烁，该音箱可能已被另外一台手机连上，则先把该手机断开蓝牙连接（也可以长按4秒播放/暂停键）
If the indicator shows blue after turning on and does not flash, the speaker may have been connected to another phone, disconnect
the phone from Bluetooth first or long press the play/pause for 4 secs.
