NEED HELP?
# 需要帮助？

## 1. If Wi-Fi setup fails:
Open the Bluetooth interface in the Divoom App and search for the Bluetooth name. If
you can't find it, press the back button of the speaker while it is showing network setup
interface. Briefly press the joystick to access the menu, enter "Settings" to reset the
network. Then search for Bluetooth again. If failed, follow these steps:
a.Make sure the device and phone are near the router
b.Open Wi-Fi networks without a password are not supported
c.Try a different phone or Wi-Fi network
d.Enure you're using the latest version of the Divoom app

## 1.网络配置失败打开点音App蓝牙界面搜索蓝牙名称，若找不到，请在音箱配网界面按返回键，短按摇杆进入菜单栏找到设置，进行重置网络。再重新搜索蓝牙，若失败，则按以下步骤：
a.确认设备及手机处于路由器附近
b.不支持无密码的网络
c.请用不同手机尝试连接，以及连接不同无线网络
d.请在应用商城下载最新版本‘点音’APP

## 2. Troubleshooting:
a.If you cannot connect to Wi-Fi, check signal quality. Keep both device and phone
near the router.
b.A firmware update may be required after connection. Tap “Update” in the app and
wait for the update to complete.
c.If the device cannot be powered off, please hold its on/off button for 8 secs to
restart. If failed, please insert a needle into the reset hole to force a shut-down.
d.If the device cannot be powered on, it could be low-powered. Please charge it for half
an hour and then try turning it on again.

## 2.排查故障
a.无法连接网络，无线网络是否良好使用，网络连接需手机和设备靠近无线网络设备。
b.正在下载固件数据，设备连接成功可能会进行固件升级，请点击升级耐心等待。
c.设备无法关机，请长按开机键8秒尝试重启。若失败，可使用顶针插入复位孔强行关机。
d.设备无法开机，可能是设备电量不足，请充电半小时后再尝试开机。
