## Questions Before You Choose Ditoo-Pro?

### What is Divoom Ditoo-Pro?

Divoom Ditoo-Pro is a retro pixel art Bluetooth speaker with a customizable pixel display, 15W sound, app-controlled animations, alarm tools, and multiple playback options. It is designed for desk setups, gaming rooms, creative spaces, and gift moments.

### Can I customize the Ditoo-Pro screen?

Yes. You can use the Divoom app to create pixel art, choose animations, display text, and browse in-app pixel designs. It lets you match the screen to your desk mood, gaming setup, or daily routine. Available features may vary by app version and device settings.

### How loud is Ditoo-Pro?

Ditoo-Pro features 15W output with DSP tuning, making it suitable for near-field desk listening, small rooms, study time, gaming setups, podcasts, and everyday music. It is built for clear daily listening rather than large outdoor parties.

### Does Ditoo-Pro support Bluetooth?

Yes. Ditoo-Pro supports wireless Bluetooth playback. It also supports TF card playback and USB-C connection for compatible laptops and desktop computers, giving users more ways to play music at home or on a desk.

### Is Ditoo-Pro good for desk setups?

Yes. Its retro computer-inspired design, pixel art display, compact body, and Bluetooth audio make Ditoo-Pro a strong fit for work desks, gaming setups, study desks, shelves, and creative rooms.

### Is Ditoo-Pro a good gift?

Yes. Ditoo-Pro is a creative tech gift for people who enjoy music, pixel art, retro design, gaming setups, desk decor, or playful electronic gadgets. It feels more personal than a standard Bluetooth speaker because the screen can be customized with pixel art and animations.

### Does Ditoo-Pro include a charging cable?

Yes. Ditoo-Pro includes the original USB charging cable in the box. A wall adapter is usually not included; use a suitable USB wall adapter for normal charging if your computer USB port does not provide enough power.
