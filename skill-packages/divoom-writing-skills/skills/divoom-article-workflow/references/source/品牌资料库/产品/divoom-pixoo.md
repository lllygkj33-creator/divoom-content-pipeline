Meet Pixoo-16

The Pixoo-16 Clock isn't just any clock. Featuring a 16x16 LED panel capable of showcasing an astonishing 16 million RGB colors, it transitions from a unique digital clock to dynamic GIF animations and pixel art, crafting a visual feast for homes, offices, and gaming rooms.

Versatility Beyond Imagination

A true master of time management, it functions as an alarm clock, scheduler, message board, and a way to mark special occasions. With added features like a countdown timer, stopwatch, scoreboard, Music Visualizer and noise detection, the Pixoo-16 brings added color and innovation to daily life.

Intelligent WiFi Connectivity

Connecting via WiFi not only ensures accurate time, but also allows you to control it remotely, sending animations and adjusting the real-time counter display anytime, anywhere. At the same time you can use this led clock panel to get and present online information, such as presenting stock exchange rates, social media followers and personal gaming data!

Personalized Pixel Artistry

Using the dedicated Divoom app designed for Pixoo-16, select or craft your animations, texts, and patterns. Whether it's a welcome message, festive celebrations, or shop decor, you have the freedom to customize the display to your taste.In addition, the Divoom App has a huge gallery and community where you can download and share your drawings, as well as communicate and interact with pixel art enthusiasts from around the world.
