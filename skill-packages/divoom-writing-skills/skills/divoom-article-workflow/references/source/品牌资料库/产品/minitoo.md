## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What is Divoom MiniToo?

MiniToo is a retro PC-style Bluetooth speaker with a smart pixel screen. It combines music playback, customizable pixel faces, clock display, alarm functions, white noise, and desk setup decoration in one compact device.

### Can I customize the MiniToo screen?

Yes. You can use the Divoom app to choose retro clock faces, animations, text, and pixel designs. Available features may vary by app version and device settings.

### Does MiniToo support Bluetooth?

Yes. MiniToo supports Bluetooth 5.3 for wireless audio. It also supports USB audio and TF card playback.

### Can I use MiniToo as an alarm clock?

Yes. MiniToo includes alarm functions, on-screen reminders, white noise, rain sounds, and Pomodoro timers.

### Is MiniToo good for desk setups?

Yes. Its compact retro PC-style design, pixel screen, clock faces, and Bluetooth audio make it suitable for work desks, gaming setups, study desks, and bedside tables.

### Is MiniToo a good gift?

Yes. MiniToo is a creative tech gift for people who enjoy music, retro design, pixel art, desk setups, gaming rooms, or cute everyday gadgets.
