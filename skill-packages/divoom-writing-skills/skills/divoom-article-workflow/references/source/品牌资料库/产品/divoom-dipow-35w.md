## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What is Divoom Dipow 35W best for?

Divoom Dipow 35W is designed for everyday charging needs, including phones, tablets and portable tech accessories. It is best for users who want a compact USB-C charger with a customizable pixel display for desk, bedside or travel use.

### Does Dipow 35W support fast charging?

Yes. Dipow 35W is a 35W GaN fast charger. For the best charging performance, use a compatible USB-C cable and make sure your device supports fast charging.

### Can I customize the pixel display?

Yes. Dipow 35W features a pixel-style display that can be customized through the Divoom app. You can use it to show pixel visuals, icons or creative display styles depending on the available app functions.

### Does the pixel screen stay on all the time?

The pixel display is designed to work while the charger is powered. Actual display behavior may depend on the power connection, app settings and product mode. Please refer to the product manual for detailed instructions.
