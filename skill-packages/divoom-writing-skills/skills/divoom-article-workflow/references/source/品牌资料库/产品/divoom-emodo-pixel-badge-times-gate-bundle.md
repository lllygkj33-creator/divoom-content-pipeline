# Divoom Emodo Pixel Badge + Times Gate Bundle

**Divoom Emodo Pixel Badge + Times Gate Bundle** \- carry your pixel art on a wearable badge, then command your desk with a five-screen pixel-art info display. Two app-controlled Divoom devices for a true desktop dashboard.

### Highlights

- **Wearable Pixel-Art Badge:** The full-color Divoom Emodo badge packs 4GB of memory (up to 6,000 images) so you can carry animations, icons, text, lyrics, and your own pixel creations, then clip it to a bag, jacket, or lanyard and switch your look in seconds.
- **Five Full-Color LCD Screens:** Times Gate packs 5 independent 128x128 LCD screens that show live data, animations, and widgets at the same time.
- **Your Desktop Dashboard:** Track stocks, crypto, YouTube subscribers, Twitch followers, weather, world clocks, and calendar events - all at a glance.
- **Immersive Ambient Lighting:** Dual-edge ambient lighting with 12 customizable RGB effects adds an immersive glow to any gaming setup or workspace.
- **App-Powered Personalization:** Design every screen in the free Divoom app and pull from thousands of community-made clock faces, GIFs, and pixel art.
- **Built for Gamers & Streamers:** A metal-framed, always-on command center for gaming rooms, streamer desks, and anime/ACG setups.
- **Two Devices, One Bundle:** Get the wearable Emodo badge and the Times Gate display together - a standout gift for geeks and pixel-art lovers.
