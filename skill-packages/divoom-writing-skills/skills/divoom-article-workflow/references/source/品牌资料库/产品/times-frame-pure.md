# Divoom Times Frame Pure Floating Smart Desk Display

The Divoom Times Frame Pure is a floating digital photo frame with a proprietary 3-layer optical structure — tempered glass, transparent LCD, and nano light-guide backplate — that makes photos appear to lift off the screen with immersive 3D depth. Factory color calibration delivers true-to-life tones for portraits and landscapes without RGB ambient lighting.

With 32GB storage (280,000+ photos or 2,400 minutes of video), WiFi 6 remote sharing, and 50+ smartwatch-style apps including 400+ dynamic clock faces, weather, stocks, calendars, and notes, Times Frame Pure works as both a digital picture frame and a modular desktop dashboard. Control it via the Divoom App or the built-in rotary knob.

Send photos, videos, and messages from anywhere through WiFi 6, customize the startup screen, and explore the 3-million-user pixel art community with 10M+ artworks. Times Frame Pure is the perfect personalized gift — for birthdays, housewarmings, long-distance relationships, or anyone who wants a smarter way to display memories.
