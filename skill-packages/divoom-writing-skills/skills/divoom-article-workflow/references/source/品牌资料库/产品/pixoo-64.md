## Frequently Asked   Questions

### What can Pixoo-64 display?

Pixoo-64 is a 10.3-inch 64x64 Wi-Fi pixel display for pixel art, animations, clocks, pixel messages, themed scenes, visual widgets, and supported social counters. It is best for pixel-style decor and creator desk displays, not for high-resolution photo viewing.

### Does Pixoo-64 need Wi-Fi and the Divoom app?

Yes. The Divoom app is the normal way to set up Pixoo-64, browse community pixel art, upload images or GIFs, change clocks and widgets, and manage the display. Wi-Fi is needed for online content updates and connected features, so avoid public Wi-Fi networks that require a web login.

### Can Pixoo-64 show social media counters or widgets?

Yes. Pixoo-64 is designed for social counters, clocks, pixel art, and dashboard-style visual widgets. Available widgets and supported platforms may depend on the current Divoom app version, account setup, and regional service availability, so check the app before buying it for one specific platform.

### Is Pixoo-64 a photo frame or Bluetooth speaker?

No. Pixoo-64 is a Wi-Fi pixel art LED display. Choose [Times Frame](https://divoom.com/products/times-frame) if you want a photo and memory display, or [Ditoo-Pro](https://divoom.com/products/divoom-pro) if you want a Bluetooth speaker with a smaller pixel screen.

### How big is Pixoo-64, and can I place it on a desk or wall?

Pixoo-64 uses a 10.3-inch 64x64 LED panel and is designed for desks, shelves, or wall-style setups. It needs continuous USB power during use. If you need a replacement stand or accessory support, [contact us](https://divoom.com/pages/contact-us) with your product model and a photo of the part.

### Can I use Pixoo-64 with Home Assistant, APIs, or custom firmware?

Pixoo-64 is designed for official use through the Divoom app. Some advanced users research third-party dashboards or developer integrations, but support can vary by firmware, app version, and setup. If you need Pixoo-64 for a specific API, Home Assistant, or custom integration, [contact us](https://divoom.com/pages/contact-us) before buying so we can route the question correctly.

### Does Pixoo-64 include a charging cable?

Yes. Pixoo-64 includes the original USB power cable in the box. A wall adapter is usually not included; for stable continuous display, use a suitable USB wall adapter instead of relying on a low-power computer USB port. If you want an extra original cable as a backup, [contact us](https://divoom.com/pages/contact-us) with your product model.
